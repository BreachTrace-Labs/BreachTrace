# BreachTrace research source of truth

**Decision, 19 November 2026.** Study the reliability of credential-specific conclusions when multiple credentials share one identity. Follow an observation from the original audit record through the timeline, incident score and published report. A correct badge on one screen does not establish that a later conclusion is equally well supported. The proposed intellectual contribution is a method and benchmark for measuring this loss of specificity, plus an empirical account of where it occurs. Its usefulness and external validity require independent evaluation.

**Critical cross-surface distinction.** The incident endpoint attaches `gcp_key_attribution` when serializing events. The pipeline passes `after_exposure(classified_events, exposure_start)` to impact/report construction. The dynamic summary uses `within_exposure` and can apply an end bound. An incident score may legitimately summarize an account. Whether a reader-facing assertion wrongly attributes that score to a key depends on its wording, scope and supporting records. Score that separately from correct account-level descriptions. See EXP-02/03.

## Model boundary

Investigator has an exposure signal, a scoped reader, a finite provider record set and a clock. Ordinary operations by another key of the same principal are a central negative control. One action can produce several records, and several actions can share a principal or resource. Record provenance means source, acquisition mode, digest and transformation history. Legal chain of custody would require additional controls. Precision, coverage, selective error, yield and the release decision are defined in the objectives and experiment plan. Open questions are how often source records identify keys, where specificity is lost, and whether faithful abstention leaves enough actionable evidence.

It explicitly documents limits for invented plain-word resource names. Local/model writer paths use different decoding settings (e.g. 0.2 in local model code, 0.3 in a writer path). Verify the effective provider configuration for each generation. The model benchmark cannot validate logical entailment merely by passing a string check.
